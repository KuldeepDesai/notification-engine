Notifier: delivery state model

The question: do the rules we agreed in CONTEXT.md still hold up when you push real cases through them? Those rules are: the audience is expanded at activation; there is one delivery per recipient per linked machine; a delivery moves Queued → Received → Displayed/Delivered → Dismissed/Actioned; expiry stops new deliveries but not things already on screen; open notifications come back after a restart until expiry; info and task respect do-not-disturb while announcements ignore it; staged notifications can be withdrawn by anyone holding the identifier; and a recipient's outcome is the furthest stage reached on any of their machines.
